Safety

  • Report someone: open their profile, tap the menu, then Report. We review reports within 24 hours.
  • Block someone: open their profile, tap the menu, then Block. They can't see your tables or profile, and you won't see theirs.
  • In danger? Contact local emergency services first.

Common questions

Why don't I see any tables?

Tables are shown near your home area. Check your home area in Profile → Edit profile. If nobody nearby has posted yet, post a table yourself.

Can other people see where I live?

No. Others see only your neighborhood, and map pins show an approximate spot, never your address.

How do I change my name or photo?

Profile → Edit profile.

How do I turn off notifications?

Profile → Settings → Notifications, or in iOS Settings.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Account → Delete account. This is permanent. Tables you host are cancelled and seats you hold are given back. You can also email us from the address you signed in with. See the privacy policy for what is deleted.
