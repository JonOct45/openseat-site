Open Seat Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Open Seat (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. We collect only the information necessary to facilitate local matchmaking for tabletop games. We do not sell your personal data, run advertisements, or track you across other applications.

Open Seat is operated by Jonathan McGrath. If you have any questions, please contact us at: jonathan.mcgrath.45@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to operate the app:

  • Account Data: Your email address, name, and authentication details (via Google or Apple Sign-In) to secure your account. Your name is never shown to other members.
  • Profile Data: Your screen name, optional profile photo, and any preferences you choose to share (e.g., pronouns, play style, bio), and your confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Location Data: We collect approximate location data (e.g., postal code or neighborhood) to show tables near you. We do not track your precise GPS coordinates.
  • App Activity: Your hosted tables, joined games, seat requests, friend lists, block/hide records, and reports you submit, to operate the matchmaking service and maintain community safety.
  • Device & Notification Data: Push notification tokens, if you opt in to receive alerts.

Note: Device permissions for Location, Camera/Photos, and Notifications are entirely optional and only accessed with your explicit consent.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Facilitate local tabletop matchmaking and community features.
  • Send essential account and game-related notifications.
  • Review reports and enforce community safety guidelines.

3. Who Can See Your Information

  • Other Users: Other members can see your chosen screen name, profile details, and the general neighborhood of your home area. Your email address is never shown to other members. To protect your privacy, any map markers associated with you or your tables are intentionally offset from your actual location.
  • Service Providers: We share necessary data with trusted third-party service providers (cloud hosting and storage, sign-in with Apple and Google, and map services) solely to operate the app. They process data only on our behalf and are required to protect it at least as well as this policy does. When you view the map, your device loads map images directly from our map provider, which receives your IP address and the map area you are viewing.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our members or others.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account data as long as your account is active. Game history (tables) is retained so participants can view past games.

Account Deletion: You may delete your account at any time within the app (Profile → Account → Delete account) or by emailing us from the address you signed in with. Upon deletion:

  • Your profile, photo, friends, invites, and active requests are permanently deleted.
  • Your hosted tables are canceled, and your name/photo will appear as "Former member" to others.
  • If you signed in with Apple, Open Seat's access to your Apple ID is revoked.
  • Safety Exception: We securely retain anonymized identifiers tied to blocks, hides, and safety reports to prevent abuse and protect the community.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. We implement strict, account-level access controls so members can only read information the app is explicitly designed to show them.

6. Children's Privacy

Open Seat is restricted to individuals 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover a minor has created an account, we will promptly delete it.

7. Your Rights

You can view, edit, or delete most of your personal data directly within the app. To request a complete copy of your data, or to exercise additional privacy rights provided by your local jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), please email us.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page, and for significant changes, we will notify you directly within the app.
