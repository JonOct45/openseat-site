Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Open Seat helps people find Commander games nearby. We collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

Open Seat is made by an independent developer, Jonathan McGrath (“we”, “us”). Questions about your data: jonathan.mcgrath.45@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and name from Apple or Google when you sign in. We use them to log you in and to contact you about your account.
  • Profile: the screen name, photo, and details you choose to add.
  • Approximate location: the neighborhood you set as your home area. We never store your exact location or track it.
  • Activity: tables you post or join, requests, friends, blocks, and reports you send.
  • Age confirmation: that you're 18 or older.

We don't collect contacts, payment information, or advertising identifiers, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

Only to run the app: showing tables near you, connecting players, sending you notices about your tables, and keeping the community safe. We don't sell or rent your data, or use it for advertising.

What other members see

  • Your screen name, photo, the profile details you add, and your neighborhood.
  • Your tables, shown only to the audience you choose. Map pins show an approximate spot, never your address.
  • Never your email address or your real name.

Who we share it with

We use a few service providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase: hosting, sign-in, and storage.
  • Apple and Google: signing in. Apple also delivers notifications and looks up neighborhood names.
  • MapTiler: map images. Your device requests them directly, which shares your IP address and the area of the map you're viewing.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Permissions

Location, camera, photos, and notifications are all optional, and are used only when you choose a related feature. You can change them any time in iOS Settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. Notifications are deleted after 90 days.

You can delete your account any time in the app (Profile → Account → Delete account), or by emailing us from the address you signed in with. Your profile and personal information are deleted, tables you host are cancelled, and if you signed in with Apple, Open Seat's access to your Apple ID is removed. We keep a limited record of reports and blocks, without your name or email, only to handle safety problems.

Your rights

You can see and edit most of your data in the app. To get a copy, correct it, or have it deleted, email us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have more rights, and we'll honor them.

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us if you spot a problem.

Children

Open Seat is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
