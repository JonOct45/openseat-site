Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Open Seat (“the app”, “we”) helps people find Commander games nearby. We collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Open Seat is made by an independent developer, Jonathan McGrath (“we”, “us”), who is responsible for your data. Questions: jonathan.mcgrath.45@gmail.com.

In short

  • Account data: the app uses Supabase for sign-in, so we collect your email address to log you in. Other members see only the screen name you choose. We never show them your real name or email.
  • Location data: we do not track or expose your precise GPS coordinates. We use geohashing to approximate your location to an area about 1 km across, for local matchmaking, so your exact whereabouts stay private.
  • In-app use only: the data we collect is used only to operate the app and help players connect. We do not sell your data to any third parties.

We don't collect contacts, browsing history, payment information, or advertising identifiers, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Who can see what

  • Other members see your screen name, photo, pronouns, profile details, and the neighborhood name of your home area.
  • Your tables are shown to the audience you choose: everyone nearby, friends only, or people you invite.
  • Map pins are moved 150–500 meters away from the center of your area, so your home can't be found from them.
  • Your email address is never shown to other members.

Services we use

The app is built with Flutter for iOS. These providers process data on our behalf to run it, under their own security standards:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, sign-in, and photo storage.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: verify your identity when you sign in.
  • MapTiler: provides map images. Your device requests map tiles directly, which shares your IP address and the map area you are viewing.
  • Apple: turns a location or postal code into a neighborhood name, and delivers push notifications.

Permissions

  • Location (optional): used once, only when you choose to set your home area from your current location. You can type a postal code instead.
  • Camera and photos (optional): used only when you choose a profile photo.
  • Notifications (optional): you can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

How long we keep data

  • Your account data is kept while your account exists.
  • Tables are kept after they end so hosts and players can see their history.
  • Notifications are deleted after 90 days.
  • When you delete your account, see below.

Community safety

We review every report and complaint about safety within 24 hours, and may limit who can join to keep the community safe. Reports and block records are used only for that purpose.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Account → Delete account), or email jonathan.mcgrath.45@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile, photo, friends, invites, and requests are deleted.
  • Tables you host are cancelled, and seats you hold are given back.
  • Your name and photo are replaced with “Former member” in other people's notifications.
  • We keep a record of blocks and hides involving your account, as account IDs only with no name or email, so we can review safety problems. Reports you sent or received are kept for the same reason.

Your rights

You can see and edit most of your data in the app. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or ask us to delete it, email jonathan.mcgrath.45@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have further rights under local law, and we will honor them.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Access to it is restricted by account, so members can read only what the app is designed to show them.

Children

The app is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app.
