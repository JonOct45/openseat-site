Open Seat

Find a Commander game nearby. Post a table when you have open seats, or find one in your area and ask to sit in.

How it works

  • Post a table: when, how many seats, and which brackets you're playing.
  • Find a game: browse open tables near your home area on a list or map.
  • Play with friends: add friends, share tables only with them, or invite people directly.

Privacy first

Your exact location is never shown. Tables appear by neighborhood, and map pins are moved a few hundred meters from your area. Read the privacy policy.
